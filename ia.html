02The four criteria

Each criterion is worth 6 marks.

Research design6 marks

A focused research question in context, a clear and justified methodology, and control of variables, so that someone else could repeat your investigation.

Data analysis6 marks

Clear, accurate recording and processing of data, including uncertainties, with appropriate graphs and statistics that are relevant to the research question.

Conclusion6 marks

A conclusion that answers the research question, is fully supported by your analysed data, and is compared with accepted scientific context.

Evaluation6 marks

Specific weaknesses and limitations of your method, how they affected your results, and realistic, relevant improvements and extensions.
